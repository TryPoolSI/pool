# Product Manager: home-coverage-first

## User stories
- As a guardian on my phone, I see in the first screen whether any joined ride in the next 7 days needs a driver, and how many rides are waiting on me.
- As a guardian who drives, I can offer to drive from a ride card that tells me when, where, how many riders and which restraints, and what happens after I offer.
- As a coordinator, I can tell a fully covered circle apart from one with no rides.
- As a keyboard or screen-reader user, opening and cancelling the offer form keeps my place.

## Scope
In: the summary, the reorder, Ride cards, the covered-circle green state, offer-form focus/heading/next-step/Cancel, removing the vehicles card, the shell tagline cleanup, a quiet refresh, and the membership error notice.
Out: see the spec's "Out of scope".
